What would I learn about ambition and isolation?

Frankenstein treats ambition as a force that becomes dangerous when it outruns responsibility. Victor's desire to defeat death isolates him from family, community, and finally from the being he creates. The novel argues that knowledge is not inherently destructive—but pursuing it without care for its human consequences can be.
